## How are settings saved?

Changes are applied only when you press Save; Cancel discards them. While something is not saved, "Unsaved changes" is shown, and leaving Settings asks whether to save, discard or stay. If a save is refused, nothing is changed.

## The pages

- **General**: interface language, appearance (theme, side panel), date and time, updates, first-run setup.
- **Files & access**: workspace, external folders, Read only / Full access.
- **Model & engine**: Luna Engine, models, speed mode, sleep mode, meaning search, cloud AI, priority, Advanced.
- **Knowledge**: About you, memories, document index.
- **Data & privacy**: what Luna stores and where; Open data folder; the Web setting (Off, Ask first, Allowed); the Web search card (service, key, option to try your other services).
- **About Luna**: version, open-source components, Copy system info (no paths or personal data).

## Date and time

Luna always knows today's date, the weekday, the local time and the time zone of this computer. In General → Date and time you can turn on "Tell Luna the Hijri date"; it stays off until you turn it on. The Hijri date follows the Umm al-Qura calendar and may differ by a day from the local crescent sighting.

## Danger zone

Actions that cannot be undone sit in a separate "Danger zone" and ask for confirmation: deleting the cloud key or a model file (Model & engine), and rebuilding or clearing the document index (Knowledge).
