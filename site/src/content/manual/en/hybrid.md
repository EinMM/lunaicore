## What is Hybrid?

With Luna Engine as the engine and the cloud AI set up, Luna can use both. The setting is Settings → Model & engine → Priority.

## Which priority should I choose?

- **Local**: everything on your computer. The cloud only when you click ☁️.
- **Balanced**: starts with the local engine. The cloud steps in when the local engine is unavailable, or when your PC can only run it on the CPU.
- **Cloud first (lighter on your computer)**, called **Lighten the PC** in the quick settings: the cloud for every message that is not sensitive; Luna Engine runs only when needed.

💡 Tip: while gaming, choose Lighten the PC so your graphics card stays free for the game.

## Sensitive messages

In Hybrid mode, messages Luna detects as sensitive stay on your PC. This detection helps, but it may not catch everything. A message counts as sensitive when, for example, it looks like it holds a password, it needs your memories while sending them is off, it has attachments without your file consent, or the conversation is private. Luna never sends a message she detected as sensitive to the cloud without your answer.

## Send one message to the cloud or to the computer

- ☁️ **Send this to the cloud**: only this message goes to the cloud; the chosen engine does not change.
- 🖥️ **Send locally**: shown when messages go to the cloud; sends only this message to the local engine, and none of it goes to the cloud.

## Private conversations

The **Private** 🔒 button makes a conversation private: it never goes to the cloud, not even with ☁️.

## How do I know where a reply came from?

A reply from the cloud carries the provider's name. When Luna sent a message to the cloud automatically, a short line says why.
