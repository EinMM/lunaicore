## Faster replies

- Choose a smaller chat model: it answers faster and puts less load on your computer.
- Turn on speed mode if your chat model has a matching speed file (see [Local engine](/docs/desktop/local-engine/)).
- Keep your models on an SSD: the model loads much faster than from a hard disk.
- Start a new conversation for each new topic: short conversations are answered faster and stay focused.

## While gaming or under heavy load

- While gaming, choose Lighten the PC in the quick settings (the model button in the message box): the cloud does most of the work and your graphics card stays free.
- Sleep mode frees graphics card memory when a game or full-screen program runs, or after an idle time you choose.
- Meaning search on the processor leaves the graphics card for chat and games.

## Better answers

- If a reply missed the point, edit your last message instead of adding a new one.
- For a question about Luna herself, start your message with /help: the answer then comes from the built-in guide only.
- Say plainly what you want, and name the file when you mean a file.

## Privacy

- Make a conversation private 🔒 before you talk about sensitive topics such as health, money or family: it never goes to the cloud.
