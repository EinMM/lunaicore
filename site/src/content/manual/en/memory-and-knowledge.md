## How do I make Luna remember something?

Say it plainly: "Remember that my favourite colour is blue." Luna shows a confirmation window before she saves. She never saves anything on her own, and she refuses passwords, codes and card numbers.

## How do I see or delete memories?

Ask "What do you remember about me?", or ask Luna to forget something. You can also delete memories in Settings → Knowledge → Memories; after a confirmation, the memory is deleted right away.

## What is "About you"?

Settings → Knowledge → About you: your name, what Luna should know about you, and how she should respond. Luna reads it with every message. It is kept on this computer only. These are preferences only: they never remove confirmations or file permissions.

## How does Luna search my documents?

Luna keeps a document index of the workspace and of the enabled folders. Ask, for example, "Search my documents for the rent contract". The answer names the file of each fact. With a meaning model chosen, Luna also searches by meaning; otherwise by words.

## The document index

Settings → Knowledge → Document index shows the number of files and parts. In the danger zone, "Rebuild index" removes what belongs to deleted files and indexes again, and "Clear index" deletes only the index. Your files are never touched.
