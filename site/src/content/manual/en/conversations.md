## How do I start a new conversation?

Press New Conversation or Ctrl+N. Each conversation opens in its own tab, and the previous one is saved to Chat History.

💡 Tip: start a new conversation for each new topic; short conversations get faster and more focused replies.

## How do I stop a reply?

Press Stop or Esc. A tool that is already running is not interrupted, but nothing new starts after you stop.

## How do I edit a message?

Point at your message and choose Edit message. Save sends it again and Luna replies again. Actions from the earlier reply are not undone.

## How do I redo a reply?

Choose Redo reply under Luna's reply: Local, Cloud or Deep think. A redo writes text only and takes no new actions.

## Titles

Luna gives each conversation a short title, made on this computer. Without a local engine, the title comes from your first message.

## Chat History

Open Chat History from the navigation rail, or press All conversations under the list in the side bar, which shows the latest 15. Double-click a conversation to open it. Use Select to delete several at once. Deleting a conversation does not touch Luna's memory or settings.

## The side panel

The side panel shows the conversation context, the available tools and Luna's activity since she started.
